Privacy Policy

Locked & Off: Leaving Check · Last updated September 30, 2026

Short version: Locked & Off doesn't collect any data. Your photos, checklists and location stay on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.

Photos

The photos you take in the app are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. They are not uploaded anywhere, not saved to your photo library and excluded from iCloud backups. They are deleted automatically after 24 hours (or after the period you choose with Premium: 1, 7 or 30 days), and you can delete them anytime in Settings.

Location

If you turn on the optional leave-home reminder, the app stores the home location you choose on your iPhone and uses the geofencing built into iOS to notice when you leave that area. Your location is never sent to us or anyone else.

Notifications

Notifications are only used for the leave-home reminder, and are created on your iPhone.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
