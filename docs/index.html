How it works

  1. When you leave, tap I'm leaving (or use Siri, the Action Button, Back Tap or Control Center).
  2. The camera walks you through your checklist: door, stove, iron and anything else you add. About 5 seconds in total.
  3. Later, glance at your Lock Screen or widget: "Home checked · 8:14 AM", with each photo stamped with the time.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never uploaded and never saved to your photo library. Old photos delete themselves automatically: after 24 hours in the free version, or after 7 or 30 days with Premium.

How do I delete all my photos?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Delete all photos. Deleting the app also removes everything.

What does Premium include?

Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds unlimited checklists (Office, Car, Vacation, Airbnb check-out and your own), unlimited items, a reminder when you leave home, and photo history for 7 or 30 days. The free version includes one checklist with up to 6 items, widgets and the Lock Screen banner.

I bought Premium but it isn't unlocked. How do I restore it?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The Lock Screen banner doesn't appear.

Go to iPhone Settings → Locked & Off and make sure Live Activities is on. The banner stays for up to 8 hours after a check.

How does the leave-home reminder work?

With Premium, you can set your home location in the app. When your iPhone notices you leaving that area, it reminds you to run your check. This needs location access set to Always and notifications turned on. Your location never leaves your phone.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit or Customize, then add the Locked & Off widget.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. The app works fully offline.
